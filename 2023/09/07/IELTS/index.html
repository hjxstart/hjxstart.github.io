8大词性

认识句子的 "零部件"
## 1. 名词 (Noun)
缩写: n. 功能: 命名万物
例子: Coffee (咖啡) Manager (经理)
## 2. 动词 (Verb)
缩写: v. 功能: 表示动作/状态
例子: Work (工作) Like (喜欢)
## 3. 形容词 (Adjective)
缩写: adj. 功能: 修饰名词
例子: Busy (忙碌的) Great (伟大的)
## 4. 副词 (Adverb)
缩写: adv. 功能: 修饰动/形
例子: Quickly (快速地) Very (非常)
## 5. 介词 (Preposition)
缩写: prep. 功能: 表示地点/时间
例子: In (在......里面) At (在......)
## 6. 连词 (Conjunction)
缩写: conj. 功能: 连接词句
例子: And (和) But (但是)
## 7. 代词 (Pronoun)
缩写: pron. 功能: 代替名词
例子: I (我) You (你) This (这个)
## 8. 数词 (Numberal)
缩写: num. 功能: 表示数量/顺序
例子: One (一) First (第一)

句子成分(主/谓/宾/表/定语/补语)

了解句子的 "骨架"
### 句子成分
## 句子成分介绍
# 词性与成分类比
词性是 "身份" (如: 医生), 成分是 "职位" (如: 院长). 
词性决定了单词在句子中的基本属性, 而成分则决定了单词在句子中具体承担的职责。

## 五大核心成分
# 主语(Subject): 动作的发起者(谁)
eg: "I" 在 "I work." 中是主语。
# 谓语(Verb/Predicate): 发出的动作(做了什么)
eg: "work" 在 "I work." 中是谓语。
# 宾语(Object): 动作的承受者(谁被...)
eg: "coffee" 在 "I love coffee." 中是宾语。
# 表语(Predicative): 说明主语是什么/怎么样(多在be动词后)
eg: "bitter" 在 "This coffee is bitter." 中是表语。
# 状语(Adverbial): 描述时间、地点、方式(何时/何地/如何)
eg: "yesterday" 在 "We had a party yesterday." 中是状语
## 两个补充成分
# 定语(attribute): 专门用来修饰、限定名词或代词。定语可以告诉我们这个东西是 "什么样的" 或 "哪一个"。
eg: I have a urgent task. urgent 修饰 task.
# 补语(compliment): 补充说明宾语(或主语)的状态。如果没有补语，句子意思不完整。[主补/宾补]
eg: I found the book interesting.
interesting 是补语，补充说明 "书怎么样"。
eg: Coffee keeps me awake.
awake 是 me 的补语。

5种简单句

### 简单句
## 1. 主谓
# S + V (主 + 谓) 
动作不涉及他人，主语自己完成动作（不及物动词）。
eg: I work.
	Birds fly.
# 不及物动词和及物动词
及物动词: 能够直接接宾语, visit your school
不及物动词: 不需要接宾语 + 或者需要借助介词接宾语, you cry(你哭)

### 2. 主谓宾
# S + V + O (主 + 谓 + 宾)
动作必须有一个承受对象 (及物动词).
eg: I love coffee.
	She reads books.
# 系动词: 
Be动词: am, is, are, was, were, been
感官动词: hear, see, watch, listen to, taste, smeall, feel, observe
表示变得...: become

### 3. 主系表
# S + V + C (主 + 系 + 表)
动词起 "连接" 作用，用来说明主语的状态或身份 (系动词)
场景系动词: be (所有形式) + 感官动词  + become (表示 “变得” 意思是相关词汇)
eg: She is happy.
	The food tastes good.

### 4. 主谓双宾 + 双宾动词
# 简单结构 D: S + V + IO + DO (主 + 谓 + 间宾 + 直宾)
动作涉及两个或两个对象，分别是 "给谁" 和 "给什么"
eg: He gave me a gift. [He gave a gift to me.]
	She taught us English.
# Object 宾语
Io=indirect object [间接宾语，人]  do=direct object [直接宾语，物]


### 5. 主谓宾 宾补
## S + V + O + C (主 + 谓 + 宾 + 宾补)
宾语后面再补充说明宾语 "变成什么/处于什么状态".
eg: They made him angry
	We call her Lucy.

## 可数名词/不可数名词
# 可数名词 (Countable)
可以数个数。注意单复数变化 (Apple Apples)。

3-九大时态–一般式/进行式/完成式

时态的重要性

## 概述
# 英语的动词时态也会发生变化
I learn English today. # 我今天学英语
I will learn English tomorrow. # 我明天学英语
I learned English yesterday # 我昨天学英语

## 为什么 "时态" 是英语的灵魂？
时态 = 时间 + 状态
# 中国学生容易忽视 "时态" 的原因:
I eat an apple today. # 我今天吃苹果
I ate an apple yesterday. # 我昨天吃苹果
I will eat an apple tomrorrow. # 我明天吃苹果

## 九大核心时态总结
时间\状态 一般式(Simple) 进行式(Continuous) 完成式(Perfect)
现在 do / does am / is / are doing have/has done
过去 did was / were doing had done
将来 will do will be doing will have done

一般式

### 一般现在时
结构: do / does (动词原形)
定义: 一般现在时用于表示客观事实、普遍真理、经常性或习惯性的动作，已经长期存在的状态。
频度副词: always(总是), usually(通常), frequently(频繁地), often(经常), occasionally(偶尔地), sometimes(有时), seldom(几乎不), never(从不).
Eg: 
	Knowledge is power. # 知识就是力量
	He often goes to the gym. # 他经常去健身房
# Comparison 比较
1. My mother said yesterday knowledge is power. # √
客观真理，不管什么时候说，都不会变。
2. My mother said yesterday konwledge was power. # ×

### 一般将来时
结构: will do / be going to do 
定义: 表示在说话时间之后将要发生的动作后状态。
表示将来的词组搭配: 1.明确的将来时间点(tomorrow,, next week/month/year etc), 2.in+时间段结构(in two days/weeks/months)
Eg: I am going to study abroad next year.

## will / be going to 的区别
# 1. be going to 时间更近，will 更远
Eg: I am going to be sick because I feel terrible now. # 我感觉要病了，因为我现在感觉不太好。
Eg: I will be sick if I eat any more of the ice cream. # 我可能要生病了，如果我吃更多的冰淇淋。
# 2. 表示 "预测" will 的语气比 be going to 要更正式
Eg: I am going to have a speech. (口语, Be going to, 更近) 
Eg: I will have a speech. (写作, Will+, 更远)

## 其他的表示将来的结构
# be to do sth 表示已经安排好要在将来发生的事，比较正式的用法。（正式）
Eg: She is to be married next months.
# be about to do sth 即将发生的动作（即将做）
Eg: You are about to finish your homework before you go to a party.

# 限制性定语从句/非限制性定语从句的区别：

原因状语从句

## 原因状语从句
# 1. 常用引导词: because, since (“既然” "已知原因"), as (解释'背景说明'), for (不能放在句首, 解释某种推断，'用逗号隔开')
# 2. 语气的强烈程度: Because > since > as
Eg: My friends dislike me 'because' I am handsome and successful.
	'Since' we don't have class tomorrow, why not go out for a picnic(野餐)?
	'As' Monday is a national holiday, all government offices will be closed.
	He is absent from class today, 'for' he must be ill.

# 3. 特殊引导词: in that(本质原因), seeing that(鉴于, 既然看到“口语化”), now that(“既然”已知原因), considering that(“考虑到”书面语), given that(“鉴于”已知前提，书面语)
Eg: E-mail is different from traditional mail 'in that' it sends and receives mails in a second.
	'Seeing that' it is raining hard, we will have to stay here for the night.
	'Now that' you are 18, you can get a driving license.

# 4. 常用的介词: because of, due to, owing to + word/phrase (位置灵活，但due to句中更常见, owing to 句首更常见),
只能接短语，不能是句子
Eg: We had an accident because he was careless.
	We had an accident due to his carelessness.
	Owing to/Because of his carelessness, we had an accident.

目的状语从句

# 1. 常用引导词: so that, in order that + 句子 (一般目的)
Eg: I turned off the TV 'in order that' my roommate could study in peace and quiet.
	We climbed high 'so that' we might get a better view.

# 2. 特殊引导词: lest(以免"lest+should+do"正式语言), for fear that(生怕), in case(万一;以防"口语化")
Eg: Take an umbrella with you 'lest' it should rain.
	People evacuated the building 'for fear that' the wall should collapse.

# 3. 简化形式: In order to / so as to do ...
Eg: We climbed high so that we might get a better view.
	'In order to' get a better view, we climbed high.
	We climbed high 'so as to' get a better view.

三大基本句型: 简单句 + 复合句 + 并列句
复合句: 定语从句(修饰名词，形容词性从句) + 状语从句(描述动作发生的'状态', 副词性从句) + 名词性从句

并列句–并列/转折/因果/选择

## 1. 表并列: and, both...and..., neither...nor...(即不...也不...), not only..., but also..., as well as
Eg: The earth is one of the sun's palnet, 'and' the moon is our satellite. (不可用两个简单句之间直接逗号隔开)


## 2. 表转折: but, while, however, nevertheless
Eg: What we are about to do is perfectly legal, 'but' please don't discuss it with anyone. 
	我们做的事情是完全合法的，但是还是不要和其他人谈及。


## 3. 表选择: or, either...or..., ranther than
Eg: Now you can have a rest 'or' you can go to the cinema.
	现在你可以休息，也可以去看电影。

## 4. 表因果: so
Eg: His eyesight was beginning to fail, 'so' he found it increasingly(越来越...) difficult to read.


## 状语从句和并列句的核心区别:
1. 状语从句是 '主从关系', 并列句是 '平等关系';
2. 状语从句: 一个主句 + 一个从句
3. 从句 '不能单独成句'
4. 从句对主句起修饰或限制作用
5. '并列句': 两个地位完全平等的简单句
6. 并列句各自 '都能独立成句'
# 同一个意思，两种句型 (状语从句/并列句)
1. 状语从句: I didn't got out because it was raining.
2. 并列句； It was raining, so I didn't go out.

### 2. 宾语从句--及物动词后(重点)
定义：在复合句中句子做宾语，就是宾语从句
## 1. 在及物动词后作宾语
Eg: I have 'learned that' everyone you meet deserves to be greeted with a smile.
	I have learned that everyone (that/whom) you meet deserves to be greeted with a smile.
	主句: 'everyone' (that/whom) you meet deserves to 'be greeted' with a smile.

## 2. 在双宾动词后作直接宾语（物）
Eg: I 'assure you that' I had no intention of offending you.
	I 'assure(保障) you that' I had no intention of offending(冒犯) you.
	I assure you sth(直接宾语)
	
## 3. 在双宾动词后做间接宾语 (人) 
Eg: They 'gave who' came to the meeting 'a pamphlet(手册)'.
	Give sb(人) sth

## 4. 在介词后做宾语 
Eg: I find the best way to concentrate is to 'make notes on what' the teachers sare saying. 
	on(介词后面) what 
	
## 5. That在宾语从句中省略的问题（在从句不做任何成分，没有词义，常省略）
Eg: Lily 'feels that' her team is better. 
	Lily feels (that,可省略) her team is better. 
	I suppose/thin （that, 可省略）... 

## 6. Whether或if引导宾语从句时，常可以互换，译为 "是否" (不能省略) 
Eg: Lily wanted to 'know if/whether' her grandma liked the handbag. 
	whether可以引导所有的名词性从句，而'if 是只能引导宾语从句',否定时不用whether.
